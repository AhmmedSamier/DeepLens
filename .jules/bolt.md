## 2024-05-24 - SearchEngine property-specific bitflags
**Learning:** In fuzzy matching hot loops (like `SearchEngine`), using an aggregate bitflag array (`itemBitflags`) for O(1) early-exit checks is great, but property-specific bitflag arrays (e.g., `itemFullNameBitflags`, `itemNameBitflags`, `itemPathBitflags`) are even better. The aggregate `itemBitflags` may pass due to characters existing across multiple different fields, causing expensive fuzzy searches to execute on a specific property when the characters aren't present in that specific property.
**Action:** When adding early-exit bitflag checks for fuzzy searching across multiple properties, create parallel property-specific bitflag arrays rather than relying solely on a single aggregate bitmask.
